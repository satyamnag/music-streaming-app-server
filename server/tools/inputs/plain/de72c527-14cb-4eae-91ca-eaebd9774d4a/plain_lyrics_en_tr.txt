Kalyanamu Loka Kalyanamu

Palla
kalyanamu loka kalyanamu,
kodandaramuniki, kaliki sitammato,
kalyanamu, yidi kalyanamu.     ||kalyanamu||

Anu
alamelu mangaku, aadhipurushuniki
venkataadri pathiki vasudha padmavatiki    ||kalyanamu||

pudami pai pantale pacha thoranamuluga,
nidivaina vrukshamula neede pandiluga
kudina tharakale velugu deepaluga
vedukaga yee pendli nedu chudandi.    ||kalyanamu||

chiru gali voople vinjamaraluga
maru malle gandhame sambrani kaga,
virula parimalamule panneeti jalluga, pakruthi
purushula pendli chudandi.     ||kalyanamu||

kokilammala pate sannaayi melamai, chi
lukamma palukulee vedamantraluga,
sakala jeeva koti pendli peddaluga,
sitaramula pendli jarige chudandi.   ||kalyanamu||

devuni pendli tanivitira chuchi,
aananda bhogaalu ponda rarandi,
sitamma, ramayya deevenalu pondi,
santhi soukyalato haayi pondandi.    ||kalyanamu||
