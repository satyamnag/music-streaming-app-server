A Wedding for the World's Well-being

Pallavi
A wedding—a wedding for the welfare of the world;
For Kodandarama and the lovely Sitamma,
A wedding—truly a divine wedding.

Anupallavi
For Alamelu Manga and the Primal Being (Adi Purusha),
For the Lord of Venkatadri and the earthly Padmavati...

With the earth's harvest serving as green festoons,
And the canopies formed by the shade of towering trees,
With the gathered stars shining as radiant lamps—
Come, witness this joyous wedding today.

With gentle breezes acting as ceremonial fans,
And the fragrance of jasmine serving as sacred incense,
With the scent of flowers like a sprinkle of rosewater—
Witness this union of Nature and the Divine.

With the song of cuckoos serving as the festive music,
And the chirping of parrots as sacred Vedic chants,
With all living beings acting as the wedding elders—
Witness the marriage of Sita and Rama taking place.

Gaze upon the Divine Wedding to your heart's content,
And come to partake in blissful joy;
Receive the blessings of Sitamma and Ramayya,
And find happiness, peace, and well-being.
