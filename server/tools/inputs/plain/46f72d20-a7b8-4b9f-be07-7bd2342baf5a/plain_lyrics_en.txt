Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!
O Lord, praised by the consort of the mountain-born Goddess (Parvati), come to our aid!
Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!

Lord Rudra (Shiva), Brahma, the Sun God, Indra,
the celestial musicians Narada and Tumburu, along with the sages—
All have arrived with devotion to serve at your feet;
Rise from your divine repose upon the serpent (Adishesha)—set aside your meditative posture (yoga mudra).
Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!

O Lord, whose vehicle is the King of Birds (Garuda), O enchanting One who sustains the universe,
O Lifter of the great mountain (Govardhana), awaken quickly and grace us with your presence!
Throughout the world, the birds, humans, and all living creatures—
Are waiting eagerly to behold your radiant countenance.
Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!

The exquisite raga 'Ananda Bhairavi' resonates,
Along with the musical essence of the sacred Vedas;
Awaken to the melodies that are a feast for the ears,
And gently open your lotus-like eyes.
Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!

O Lifter of Mount Mandara, O wish-fulfilling tree (Mandara) to those who seek refuge in You,
O dweller of Anjanadri—why this lingering slumber?
O beautiful and auspicious Lord Venkateswara,
O Lord who bears the 'Srivatsa' mark upon your chest, O sustainer of all—please arise!
Awaken, O Lord Rama of Bhadradri!
