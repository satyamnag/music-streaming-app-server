**Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya**
Adrija Pathi Vinutha Aadu Kovayya!
Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya!

Rudra, Brahmadulu, Ardhapudu, Indrudu, Na
Rada, Tumbura, Naada Ganamulu, Munulu,
Shraddhaga Nee Pada Sevaku Vachchiri,
Kadruvaamshaju Paanpu, Yoga Mudranu Veedi.
Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya!

Khaga Raja Vaahana, Jaga Melu Mohana,
Naga Raja Dhara Vega Mammela Meluko,
Jaga Muna Khagamulu, Nara, Pashu Ganamulu
Mogamunu Choodaga Munduga Vechchiri,
Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya!

Aananda Bhairavi Apuroopa Raagamu
Sannaga Vedamula Sangeetha Saaramu,
Veenula Vinduga Vinapada Meluko, Na
Yana Kamalamulu Nemmadi Vidadisi,
Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya!

Mandara Giri Dhara, Aashrita Mandaara, Anja
Naadri Vaasa Alasata Yelara
Sundara Srikara Sri Venkateswara
Srivatsya Vakshasa Sridhara Levara
Bhadradri Ramayya Nidra Levayya!
