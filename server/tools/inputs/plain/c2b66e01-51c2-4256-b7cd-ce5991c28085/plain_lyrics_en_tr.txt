{Pallavi}
maa inti daivamaa, maakanti veluga!
srikanta, srasta nutha sri venkateswara!
maa inti daivamaa, maakanti veluga!
sri venkateswaraa, maa rakshakuda!

{Anupallavi}
maa ila velpaa, maa kalpa vrukshamaa,
maa ichalu teerchu prathyaksha daivamaa!

{Charanam 1}
papapu kondalu dudila dahinchi,
thapamulu tirchedi tirumala ramanaa!
shapagrasta bratuku swarga thulyamu chesi,
kapadu kali kalpa daivamaa ramanaa!

{Charanam 2}
modu baarina bratukulu chigurimpa jesi,
thodu needaga yundu maa kanna tandri!
vadani puvvugaa maa janma marchu,
vedakane varamulidu venkata naadhaa!

{Charanam 3}
mati mangala rupa, manchi cheyutalo,
saati leni deva, sri devataa ramana!
koti janmala divya punya falamaa,
natana sutra dhari naga shayi!

{Charanam 4}
sarva subhamulu kuurchu darshanam bosage,
sarveswara hari sri srinivasaa!
urvi praanula nella samariti juche,
nirvikara roopa narayana hary!
