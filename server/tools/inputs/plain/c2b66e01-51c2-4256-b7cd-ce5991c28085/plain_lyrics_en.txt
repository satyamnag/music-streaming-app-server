{Pallavi}
O Deity of our home, O light of our eyes!
O Lord praised by Shiva and Brahma—Sri Venkateswara!
O Deity of our home, O light of our eyes!
O Sri Venkateswara, our protector!

{Anupallavi}
O tutelary deity of our land, O wish-fulfilling tree (Kalpa Vriksha),
O manifest God who grants all our desires!

{Charanam 1}
O Lord of Tirumala, who burns away mountains of sins as easily as cotton,
And dispels all our afflictions!
You transform lives burdened by curses into something akin to heaven;
O Lord of the Kali Yuga, You are our divine protector!

{Charanam 2}
O Father, who makes withered lives bloom anew,
Always remaining by our side as our constant support!
You turn our existence into an ever-fresh, unfading flower;
O Lord Venkateswara, You grant boons even before we ask!

{Charanam 3}
O Lord of supreme, auspicious form—unequaled in Your benevolence,
O beloved of the Divine!
You are the fruit of divine merit earned over countless lifetimes;
O Lord who reclines upon the serpent (Naga Shayi), You are the Master who orchestrates the cosmic play!

{Charanam 4}
You grant a vision (darshan) that bestows every form of auspiciousness,
O Lord of all, O Hari, O Sri Srinivasa!
You look upon all living beings on this earth with equal grace,
O immutable, unchanging Lord—O Narayana, O Hari!
