{Pallavi}
Ravammaa Mahalakshmi! Ravamma Sri Lakshmi!
Ravammaa Dhana Lakshmi! Ravamma Jaya Lakshmi!

{Anupallavi}
Ramaa Ramanhivi Devi, Ramya Swarupini,
Kamaniya Kantaa! Kadali Ravammaa,

{Charanam 1}
Nadakala hoyalalo nayagaaramu ninda,
Adugadugu mattha gajamulaa veyuchu,
Jadala kulukulatho Rajahamsa vole,
Vadi vadigaa Venkata Lakshmi Ravammaa.

{Charanam 2}
Kamalamu, kalasamu abhaya mudralato,
Ramaniya haramulu medalona meriyaga,
Kamaniya karuna kantulu nimpukoni,
Ramaa Devi Ravammaa, Rathanaala Gummaa.

{Charanam 3}
Swarna kinkinula ravamulu nindaga,
Sarigama swaramulu swagathamu palkaga,
Varalu vaddaanapu talukulu prabhavinchaga,
Siru lolukaga Sudati Siri Ravammaa.

{Charanam 4}
Bhavukulu, bhaktulu prardhana cheyaga,
Kavi, gayakulu keertanalu cheyaga,
Nava jeevana soukhya sanketha prabhalato,
Srivenkataa Giri Rani Ravamma Tally.
