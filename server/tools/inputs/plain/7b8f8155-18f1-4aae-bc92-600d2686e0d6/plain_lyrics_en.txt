{Pallavi}
Come, O Mahalakshmi! Come, O Sri Lakshmi!
Come, O Dhana Lakshmi! Come, O Jaya Lakshmi!

{Anupallavi}
O Goddess Rama, embodiment of loveliness,
With a melodious voice—please come forth,

{Charanam 1}
With grace and charm in your gait,
Taking each step with the majesty of an intoxicated elephant,
Swaying like a royal swan,
Come quickly, O Venkata Lakshmi.

{Charanam 2}
Bearing the lotus, the sacred pot, and the gesture of protection (Abhaya Mudra),
With beautiful necklaces gleaming upon your neck,
Radiating the light of lovely compassion,
Come, O Goddess Rama, O jewel-like one.

{Charanam 3}
Amidst the tinkling sounds of golden anklets,
As musical notes offer a melodious welcome,
With the radiant gleam of the ornate waist-belt,
Oh lovely Goddess of Prosperity, please come.

{Charanam 4}
As devotees and visionaries offer their prayers,
And poets and singers sing hymns of praise,
Radiating the glow that heralds a blissful new life,
Oh Queen of the Venkata Hill, Mother, please come.
