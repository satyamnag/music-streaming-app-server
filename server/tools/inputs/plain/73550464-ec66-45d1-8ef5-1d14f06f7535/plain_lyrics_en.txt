Come to our home, O Sri Rama Chandra.

Pallavi.
Come to our home, O Sri Rama Chandra!
Come to our home.

Anupallavi.
Sita waits ahead to offer you solace;
O Rama, destroyer of sins, please step this way!

Why hesitate to draw near gracefully?
O Lord praised by the Vedas and Agamas, please come to me.

I have beautifully strung garlands of fragrant jasmine;
O Lord praised by Sanaka and others, I bid You welcome!

With a gentle smile upon your face,
Dharanija (Sita) waits for you; please grace us with your presence!

Everyone rejoices upon beholding You;
O Lord of Indira (Lakshmi), please come now!

Descend from the hills, O Kodanda Rama!
O Lord who rides the bird-vehicle (Garuda), stand as our support.
