Pallavi (Refrain)
A game of flowers played by Rama with his beloved;
A delightful pastime enjoyed by the Lord of Prosperity—
Coming close to his charming Janaki,
Sri Ramachandra engages in this playful sport. || inthitho ||

Tanu (Charanam)
It is a game played by the Primal Lord (Adi Deva),
Who bestows wealth, the joy of progeny,
And every form of blissful fortune upon all.
Sita, the jewel among women,
Stands by his side; he gently touches her cheek,
And with playful teases—he, the father of Manmatha (the God of Love)—
Engages in this wondrous game today. || inthitho ||

Rama, with his beloved Janaki,
Holds the ball firmly and tosses it;
With a radiant, smiling face, he plays—
A delightful, spirited game of playful contention. || inthitho ||

The women laugh aloud, giggling and chatting,
Laughing heartily and chatting among themselves,
Offering lively suggestions on how to throw the ball swiftly—
A playful, flower-filled game among the companions. ||inthitho ||

Amidst the wedding festivities and the gathered women,
The divine bridegroom and his bride—
The Lord of the Universe and the Sovereign Lady—
Engage in this grand, cosmic game of ball. || inthitho ||
