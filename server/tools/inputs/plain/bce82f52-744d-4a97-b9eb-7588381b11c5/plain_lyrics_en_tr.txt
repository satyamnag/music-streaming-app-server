Anupallavi.
jaanaki, lakshmana, anuja sameta,
hanumatsevita ayodhyapatiki.   ||harathu||

bhadraadri pathi bhumija vibhuniki,
rudra foojita aadhi devuniki,
uddhruta rakshasa prana ghaathaka,
roudraakarudu ranadhiruniki.     ||harathu||

chakalakondanu challaga chuse,
srikarudu srirama chandruniki,
sakala sampadalu kollaga nicche,
akalankudu aanandaramuniki.   ||harathu||

shankara chapamu trunchina ghanudu,
pankaja janaki - pathi devuniki,
lankeswaruni mada managinchina,
shankhu, chakra, gada, krupaana dhariki.   ||harathu||

ramgapati subha gaatruniki,
manganu battina manju murtiki,
