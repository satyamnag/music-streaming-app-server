Pallavi.
Offer aarati (ceremonial lamps) to Sri Rama,
To the Lord of Sri (Lakshmi), the Supreme Srinivasa. ||Aarati||

Anupallavi.
To the Lord of Ayodhya—accompanied by Janaki (Sita), Lakshmana, and his other younger brothers—
And served by Hanuman. ||Aarati||

To the Lord of Bhadradri, the consort of Bhumija (Sita/daughter of the Earth),
To the Primordial Deity worshipped by Rudra (Shiva),
To the fierce warrior who destroyed the lives of the formidable rakshasas (demons).
To the ferocious warrior, the hero of battle. ||Aarati||

To the One who looks benevolently upon Chakalakonda,
To Sri Rama Chandra, the bestower of prosperity,
To the One who grants abundant wealth and blessings,
To the immaculate Ananda Rama. ||Aarati||

To the glorious One who broke the bow of Shankara (Shiva),
To the Lord of the lotus-eyed Janaki,
To the One who humbled the pride of the Lord of Lanka (Ravana),
To the wielder of the shankha (conch), chakra (discus), gada (mace), and kripana (sword). ||Aarati||

To the One with an auspicious, human-like form—
the Lord of Sriranga, of auspicious body,
To the charming Deity who holds Manga (Lakshmi) close,
To the Sovereign who reigns over Venkatagiri. ||Aarati||
