Specials

This panel is the source of truth for the Specials on the home screen: the app ships no built-in shelf list, so every shelf below is one you created here. Each Special is a landscape banner carousel — upload a banner to give a shelf its own artwork; without one it falls back to its tracks' cover art. Membership is the ordered track list you pick per shelf; keywords are an optional extra rule. A shelf with no tracks does not appear on the home screen at all.

Banner format — the spec to export to

Format WebP, lossy, quality 80–85, sRGB, no alpha (the banner is opaque and always fills its box), metadata stripped
Aspect ratio 8:3 (2.667:1) — exact, within 2%. Enforced on upload, because the app sizes the slide from the artwork's own ratio and never letterboxes or stretches it
Pixels 1440×540 is the export size. 1080×405 is the minimum that still looks sharp; 2160×810 is the largest worth uploading
Why 1440×540 The slide is the screen width less 12dp each side, so 336dp on a 360dp phone, and 8:3 makes it 126dp tall. At 1440px the artwork is 1:1 or better up to 4× density on a 360dp phone and up to 3.5× on a 411dp one — every mainstream phone, with no upscaling
Keep clear Right ~half: the copy sits there — “FEATURED PLAYLIST”, the shelf name (up to 2 lines), the song count and the Play Now button, all right-aligned and vertically centred.
Bottom centre: the page dots are drawn inside the artwork there.
Contrast: the app deliberately draws no dark scrim — the white copy carries its own text shadow instead — so keep the right half away from near-white and busy areas or the text will be hard to read.
Composition that works: put the subject on the left two-thirds and leave the right third calm and mid-to-dark toned. The banner is shown at full brightness, so export it looking the way you want it seen.

New shelf

A new shelf starts with no tracks, so it stays off the home screen until you add some. The id is derived from the title when you leave it blank.

Featured Playlists

The round buttons shown in a row under the home carousel. This panel is the source of truth: the app ships no built-in playlist list, so every chip below comes from here. Each chip plays the ordered tracks you pick for it; keywords are an optional extra rule. A playlist with no tracks does not appear on the home screen at all. Each playlist's icon is an image you upload (square WebP); until one is uploaded the app draws a built-in symbol.

New playlist

A new playlist starts with no tracks, so it stays off the home screen until you pick some. Keywords typed here turn keyword matching on for the new playlist; untick it on the row afterwards if you want hand-picked tracks only.

The band is 8:3, so the upload is automatically trimmed to that shape before it is stored — what you see in the preview below is exactly what the phone draws, at full brightness with the header text carrying its own shadow. Lyrics —

Build sync lyrics from an SRT Re-times an uploaded SRT against the audio and fills all three columns via GitHub Actions.
Build sync lyrics from an SRT
The Telugu lines are re-timed against the real audio, then the English translation and transliteration are rendered from the plain columns. Every uploaded line must already be an exact plain-lyric line of the chosen track. Nothing is written unless every validation passes.
Auto-Sync Lyrics

Sends this track's audio to Deepgram (nova-3, Telugu) and matches the returned word timings against the Plain Telugu lyrics. Every language column shares one timeline, so one run covers all five.
Propose only fills the editor below for review. Propose & Save writes directly to the database, but only when every single line clears the confidence bar and the track has no existing sync lyrics — otherwise it saves nothing and tells you why. Every write is verified by re-reading the row and can be rolled back.
Add Music banners fills every stretch with no singing (intro, interludes, outro) with a {{Music}} cue, so the timeline runs from 00:00:00,000 to the end of the audio, and gives a line that is sung more than once its own cue for each occurrence. Section headings like {Charanam 1} are never timed.
This is a suggestion, not a guarantee — singing makes speech models less reliable than they are on speech.

Build one column at a time

Each step fills only its own Sync tab and saves nothing. Review it, press Save, then run the next step. Step 1 uses Deepgram once; steps 2 and 3 copy the Telugu timings and never transcribe.

Markup. A line that is only {Pallavi} in Plain lyrics becomes a section divider. In Sync lyrics, a cue whose text is {{Music}} becomes a ♪ Music ♪ banner — write it as the text of a normal timed cue, for example a 00:00:10,000 --> 00:00:20,000 line followed by {{Music}} on the next line. A bare {{Music}} line with no timestamp is rejected, because nothing would tell the app when to show it. (Legacy cues that already read ♪ Music ♪ are shown as the banner too.)
