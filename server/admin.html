Specials

This panel is the source of truth for the Specials on the home screen: the app ships no built-in shelf list, so every shelf below is one you created here. Each Special is a landscape banner carousel — upload a banner to give a shelf its own artwork; without one it falls back to its tracks' cover art. Membership is the ordered track list you pick per shelf; keywords are an optional extra rule. A shelf with no tracks does not appear on the home screen at all.

New shelf

A new shelf starts with no tracks, so it stays off the home screen until you add some. The id is derived from the title when you leave it blank.

Featured Playlists

The round buttons shown in a row under the home carousel. This panel is the source of truth: the app ships no built-in playlist list, so every chip below comes from here. Each chip plays the ordered tracks you pick for it; keywords are an optional extra rule. A playlist with no tracks does not appear on the home screen at all. Each playlist's icon is an image you upload (square WebP); until one is uploaded the app draws a built-in symbol.

New playlist

A new playlist starts with no tracks, so it stays off the home screen until you pick some. Keywords typed here turn keyword matching on for the new playlist; untick it on the row afterwards if you want hand-picked tracks only.

Appearance

The home screen wallpaper: a landscape band across the top of the home page, behind the “Soulful Bhakti” header. Upload a landscape WebP (16:9 is fine; 1920×720 recommended). The band is 8:3, so the upload is automatically trimmed to that shape before it is stored — what you see in the preview below is exactly what the phone draws, at full brightness with the header text carrying its own shadow. Remove it to return to the plain themed background.

Lyrics —

Markup. A line that is only {Pallavi} in Plain lyrics becomes a section divider. In Sync lyrics, a cue whose text is {{Music}} becomes a ♪ Music ♪ banner — write it as the text of a normal timed cue, for example a 00:00:10,000 --> 00:00:20,000 line followed by {{Music}} on the next line. A bare {{Music}} line with no timestamp is rejected, because nothing would tell the app when to show it. (Legacy cues that already read ♪ Music ♪ are shown as the banner too.)
